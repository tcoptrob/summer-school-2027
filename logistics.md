## Venue
The summer school will be hosted at the [Chalmers Conference Center](https://www.chalmerskonferens.se/en/) at **Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden**. 

Chalmers is one of Sweden’s leading technical universities, consistently ranked among the top engineering institutions in Europe, with particular strength in robotics, automation, mechatronics, and control engineering. The hosting division is the Division of Dynamics, Department of Mechanical Engineering, which has direct access to lecture halls, seminar rooms, and robot laboratories on campus.

The Chalmers campus is centrally located and easily accessible by public transportation from Gothenburg city center and from Gothenburg Landvetter Airport (approximately 25 minutes by bus).
