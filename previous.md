## First Edition of the summer school on Optimization for Robotics
The first edition of this summer school was held on July 14-18, 2025 at the University of Patras, Greece. 
Over the span of 5 days, approximately 100 participants consisting of master's, PhD, and early-career researchers, attended lectures, keynotes and practicals given by high-profile speakers from institutions including NTNU, MIT, KAIST, INRIA, and the University of Trento.

<hr>
After the summer school, all materials have been made public online:
The lecture slides and other material are available [here](https://tinyurl.com/optrob25).

Code for most of the practicals is available in this [GitHub repository](https://github.com/upatras-lar/optrob25-practicals).

All the recordings from the lectures are available [here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6YqvXk7hmsk0jkSrJYq7x7oq7maJyqJ-).
<hr>
Due to the positive feedback on this summer school, the second edition will be hosted in 2027, at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg, Sweden.
