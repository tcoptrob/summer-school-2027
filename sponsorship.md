## Funding Model and Sponsorship Levels
The summer school will be funded by registration fees collected from the participants and financial support from our sponsors. The registration fees will cover the costs for accommodation, meals, transportation to the venue, etc. for every participant. The funds from our sponsors will be used to reduce registration fees, fund the hackathon hardware and prizes, and supports travel for participants who could not otherwise attend.
**In particular, 15% of all sponsorship funds will be allocated to waiving registration and lodging fees for participants from under-represented groups.**

Sponsors will gain targeted publicity throughout our full one-week event, networking and engagement opportunities with the upcoming talent in robotics. We offer various packages for sponsorship.

**Platinum Sponsor (€20,000 EUR):** (Limited to 2 partners)
+ A **20-minute splash talk** to the full cohort, plus two complimentary registrations
+ Opportunity to co-define a Day-5 hackathon challenge around your own robotics problem
+ Full-page advertisement in the event programme brochure
+ Top-tier logo placement on the main stage backdrop, website and lecture-slide template
+ All Gold, Silver and Bronze sponsorship benefits

**Gold Sponsor (€10,000 EUR):**
+ A **10-minute splash talk** to the full cohort, plus one complimentary registration
+ Dedicated social media posts acknowledging your sponsorship
+ Half-page advertisement in the event programme brochure
+ Recruiting table at the venue during coffee breaks
+ All Silver and Bronze sponsorship benefits

**Silver Sponsor (€5,000 EUR):**
+ Access to opt-in contacts of attendees for hiring
+ Quarter-page advertisement in the event programme brochure
+ Named sponsorship of a coffee break or a social event
+ All Bronze sponsorship benefits

**Bronze Sponsor (€2,000 EUR):**
+ Logo placement on promotional materials — event banners, signage and the school website
+ Recognition during the opening and closing ceremonies

We are open to discussing additional customized sponsorship opportunities to better align with your company's goals and values. This may include providing experimental equipment, cloud compute credits for the practical sessions, prize money for the winning hackathon teams, named travel grants or scholarships, or other tailor-made options.
